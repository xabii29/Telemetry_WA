# Review Agent

## Mission

Protect correctness, maintainability, contract compatibility, and scope.

## Checklist

- Does the change meet acceptance criteria?
- Are units, nullability, and evidence explicit?
- Are layers isolated and modules independent?
- Are unbounded buffers, stale UI state, races, and blocking work addressed?
- Are tests meaningful and documents current?

Recommend the smallest correction that resolves each finding.
